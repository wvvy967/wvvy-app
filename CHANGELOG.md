# Changelog

What's changed in the WVVY app.

## 1.0.1 (September 3, 2026)

- **Volume on iPhone.** A real slider under the play button — and it moves right along with your phone's volume buttons.
- **AirPlay.** Send the stream to a HomePod, an Apple TV, or any AirPlay speaker.
- **The week at a glance.** The schedule now shows every day on one screen, today first, instead of swiping across day tabs.
- **A smoother player**, with recently played tucked one tap away so the cover art stays front and center.

## 1.0.0 (September 3, 2026)

- **Tune in.** Opens on what's playing right now, one tap to start. Live, straight from the station — the same WVVY 96.7 you'd hear on the dial in Tisbury.
- **See what's on.** The song playing now, with its cover, and the handful that came before it, so you can catch the name of the thing you just missed.
- **On air, or not.** A light tells you at a glance whether WVVY is broadcasting, whether or not you're the one listening.
- **Keeps playing in your pocket.** Lock the phone, switch apps, drop it in a bag — the stream keeps going, with the track and cover on your lock screen and play controls right there.
- **Plays through the car.** Connect over Bluetooth or a cable and WVVY comes through the speakers, with the song and artist showing on the head unit.
- **Picks itself back up.** Drive through a dead spot or lose the signal for a moment and it reconnects on its own, instead of leaving you tapping play.
- **The week's shows.** A schedule of who's on when, day by day, with a badge on whoever's live right now — every show programmed by a volunteer from the island.
- **Support the station.** Give by Venmo or PayPal in a couple of taps, or send a check by mail — with the details a 501(c)(3) receipt needs.
- **Listen anywhere else, too.** Copy the stream into Sonos, VLC, a smart speaker or a car stereo, or grab the .pls / .m3u files for hardware that wants them.
- **Install it, wherever you are.** iPhone, Android, and as a web app on anything else. Installed, it opens straight away, even before the stream connects.
- **Nothing is tracked.** No accounts, no analytics. The app plays one station and asks nothing of you.
